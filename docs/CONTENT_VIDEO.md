## AI used (say it in the video, label it on screen)
- **Voiceover:** our own voices are the default (we dropped AI voice for the pitch). For the video's AI requirement, use AI auto-captions plus Sarvam Hindi TTS for the product's voice reply shown on screen.
- **Captions:** auto-captions in CapCut or Vrew, then corrected by hand.
- **Optional:** AI b-roll of an abstract ledger or paper texture. **No** generated people and no real politicians, places or party symbols.

## Shot list during the build (phone, vertical)
R0 setup · R3 first booth grid on screen · R6 the late-night grind · **R9.5–10.5 the eval reveal** (don't miss it) · R14 freeze high-five · a rehearsal.

## Look
The same look as the product ([design system v2](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md), butter.video-inspired): a near-white page, grey panels, big regular-weight Geist two-tone titles and yellow signal chips. One tactile 3D logo object is allowed on the end card. No dark-mode screen recordings, no glitch transitions and no stock "AI" visuals.

## Rules
Synthetic data only on screen. No party names, colours or symbols. No real voter names. Show the "synthetic data" banner in at least one demo shot.
