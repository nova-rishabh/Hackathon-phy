# RollWatch: Brand Brief

> **Rishabh designs the logo.** This page is the brief: what to use, what to avoid, and the asset list with exact sizes. Visual language: [DESIGN_SYSTEM.md](DESIGN_SYSTEM.md) (butter.video-inspired).
> The v1 concepts (ink fingertip, booth grid, rubber stamp, ledger serial, mirror monogram) and the violet/brick palette are **rejected**. Don't revive them.

---

## 1. Brand in one line

**RollWatch is a precise, neutral instrument for checking voter-roll changes and the rumours about them.**
- **Personality:** calm, exact, slightly playful in form (butter's tactile objects) and dead serious in content.
- **Never:** alarmist, partisan or "AI magic".

## 2. Name and wordmark

- **Written:** `RollWatch` in sentence use, `rollwatch` in the wordmark if you go lowercase. Never "Roll Watch" or "ROLLWATCH".
- **Wordmark direction (butter-like):** butter's wordmark is a custom script with a tiny dot after it (`Butter·`). For us, a custom-drawn or modified **grotesk** wordmark fits the "instrument" feel better than script. Options to try:
  1. **Geist 500, tightened**, with one custom detail: the two `l`s in "roll" drawn as **tally strokes**. Counting is what we do.
  2. **The `o` as a booth tile:** a small rounded square instead of the round `o`, in signal yellow at most.
  3. **A trailing mark**, like butter's dot: a tiny yellow square after the wordmark, `rollwatch▪`, used as the favicon on its own.

## 3. Logo object ideas (the butter "tactile object" approach)

Butter's hero is a keychain of chunky 3D objects. Our logo can be a **flat mark plus one 3D "object" version** for the share card, video and slides. Ideas, strongest first:

| # | Idea | Why it fits | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Split bar.** A short rounded bar split into 4 grey segments and one hatched-yellow end segment | It *is* our product: the "which story fits" bar. It reads at 16px. As a 3D object, it's a chunky segmented pill. | Keep the segment count at 4–5, and keep it readable at favicon size. |
| 2 | **Tally pill.** Four vertical strokes and a diagonal, inside a rounded tile | Counting and auditing. It's neutral and very Indian-ledger. | The tally alone is generic, so it needs the tile or the colour to own it. |
| 3 | **Booth tile.** One rounded square with a small notch or hatched corner | It matches the booth grid and works as an app icon. | Can look like any app icon. Make the hatch the identity. |
| 4 | **Mirror chip.** Two half-tiles with a gap (a dashed axis) | "Same treatment for both sides". It tells our neutrality story. | Needs explaining, so it's better as a secondary mark. |
| 5 | **Verdict chip as object.** A tactile 3D chip reading `CHECKED` | Great for the share card and the video end card. | Not a primary logo, since the text is too small at icon size. |

**Recommendation:** use **#1 split bar** as the mark and **#5** as the marketing object.

**Avoid** (all of these read as generic, AI-made or partisan):
- eyes, magnifiers or shields with ticks
- ballot boxes, EVMs or ink fingers (v1)
- the tricolour or saffron
- lotus, hand, broom, bicycle, elephant, clock, arrow or any election-symbol shape
- sparkles, brains or circuits
- gradient blobs

## 4. Colour and type for the brand
- **Mark:** ink `#0F0F0F` on page `#F9F9F9`, with butter yellow `#F6D84D` as the only accent, used on one element.
- **One-colour version:** all ink. **Reverse:** never needed (no dark backgrounds).
- **Type:** Geist (display, regular weight) and Geist Mono (labels, numbers).

## 5. Taglines (pick one per surface)
- "Check the roll. Check the rumour." (video hook, share card footer)
- "Which story fits?" (product hero)
- "On no one's side." (closing line)

## 7. Brand rules for content
- Synthetic data only, and the "synthetic · Sampleabad is fictional" note is visible on every external asset.
- No real politicians, parties, places or symbols, and no generated people.
- AI use is labelled in the video (voice, captions, any generated b-roll).
